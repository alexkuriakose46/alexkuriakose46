> *"I didn't choose tech — tech chose me at 3am when a bug refused to die."*

<br>

I'm **Alex**, an AI Engineer and Full-Stack Developer based in **Kerala, India**. I obsess over building things that are both intelligent and beautiful — whether that's a neural network, a slick web app, or an IoT system that talks back to you.

I genuinely believe that **great software feels alive**. It anticipates you, responds to you, and makes you smile. That's what I try to build every single day.

When I'm not in front of a screen, I'm probably thinking about what to build next.

<br>

---

<br>

## 🧠 What I actually do

Most days look something like this — I'm either training models, debugging something that "should work", designing a UI that people actually enjoy using, or building embedded systems that make physical things smarter.

I specialize in three things that don't often come in the same person:

- 🤖 **AI & Machine Learning** — TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, the whole family
- 🌐 **Full-Stack Web** — React frontends, FastAPI backends, MySQL databases
- 🔌 **IoT & Embedded Systems** — Arduino, MQTT, distributed sensor networks
